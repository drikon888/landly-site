Privacy policy

Effective from November 23, 2026

Who we are

Landly is run by Conscious Brands FeA LLC, 1309 Coffeen Ave, STE 1200, Sheridan, Wyoming, USA ("we"). We decide how your data is used in Landly and are responsible for it.

Questions and requests about your data: info@consciousbrandsfeallc.com.

The short version

Landly works without an account, without ads and without selling your data.

We keep what Landly needs to work: the flights you follow, your passport, your alert choices and your settings. Everything else is optional and off until you switch it on.

What we collect

Account: when you first open Landly, your phone gets an anonymous account (a random number). If you sign in, we also keep your email address and, with Apple or Google, the identifier they give us. With Apple you can hide your real address; we do not ask for your name.

Your flights: the flight numbers and dates you follow, whether each one is yours or a friend's, the alerts you chose for it, and the landed flights that make up your passport (airports, distance, time in the air, delay).

Settings: language, units, clock, home airport, your answers to the first-run questions (how often you fly, what worries you) and your two consents.

Limits: how many route searches you made today, to apply the daily limit.

Notifications: if you allow them, a technical token for your phone so we can send alerts.

Subscription: if you subscribe to Landly Pro, whether your subscription is active. Payment details stay with Apple or Google; we never see them.

Usage statistics: which screens and features are used (searches, followed and shared flights, the Pro screen), with a random app identifier; never your email, name, flight numbers or advertising ID. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask first and they stay off unless you agree; elsewhere they are on. You can switch them off any time in Privacy and data.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos or your boarding pass details beyond the flight number and date.

Why we use it

To provide Landly (the agreement between us when you use the app): show and follow your flights, keep them on all your phones, send the alerts you asked for, manage your subscription.

With your consent: news and offers by notification, and usage statistics in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. You can withdraw your consent any time in Settings; what happened before stays lawful. Elsewhere, usage statistics rest on our legitimate interest in improving the app, and you can switch them off.

Our legitimate interest: keeping Landly secure and fair to use, for example the daily search limit and protection against abuse.

Who else handles it

We use a few providers that process data on our behalf and only as we instruct:

Supabase (database, accounts and sign-in emails; servers in Frankfurt, Germany).

AeroDataBox, through RapidAPI, for flight data. Our server asks them about flight numbers, airports and dates; it never sends who is asking.

Apple and Google, if you sign in with them or buy a subscription through their stores.

RevenueCat, to know whether a subscription is active. It receives your anonymous account number and the store's purchase records.

Expo, to deliver notifications to your phone.

Google (Google Analytics for Firebase), for usage statistics on Android. It receives the screens used and the events listed above, with a random app identifier, never the advertising ID. Google may process these data outside your country under its standard contractual clauses.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Your account data stays until you delete your account. "Delete my account" in Privacy and data removes it from our database at once; copies in backups disappear within 30 days.

Flight information that is not about you (times, gates, positions) is kept in our cache for at most 7 days after the flight.

Your rights

You can see and download your data (Settings → Privacy and data → Download my data) and delete it (Delete my account). You can also ask us to correct it, restrict or object to its use, or send it to another service, by writing to info@consciousbrandsfeallc.com.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom you can complain to your data protection authority. Residents of California and other US states have similar rights; we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Children

Landly is not meant for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted, each account can only read its own rows, and the flight data key never leaves our server. No system is perfectly secure: if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date below, and tell you in the app when the change matters.
